Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 16, 2026

1. Summary

RK Clips Publisher is a personal tool used only by its owner. It does not collect data from other people.

2. Data the app uses

  • Account information of the owner's own accounts (account ID, username, avatar), received when the owner signs in with TikTok or Facebook, to show which account is connected.
  • Access tokens issued by TikTok and Meta, stored only on the owner's own computer, used to upload and publish the owner's videos.
  • Videos and captions created by the owner, sent to the platforms only when the owner publishes them.

3. What the app does not do

The app does not read other users' profiles, messages or content. It does not sell, share or transfer any data to third parties, and it does not use data for advertising. There are no analytics or tracking cookies on this website.

4. Storage and deletion

All data stays on the owner's computer. The owner can disconnect the app at any time in the TikTok, Instagram or Facebook security settings, which revokes the tokens, and can delete the local files.

5. Contact

Privacy questions can be sent through github.com/RKclips/RKclips.github.io/issues.
